## 1. Abstract & Fundamental Axioms

### 1.1 Abstract

This paper presents a new unified cosmological model (Entropy Wave Hypothesis) that provides a single, wave-based framework for major unsolved problems in modern physics—such as the incompatibility between general relativity and quantum mechanics, mathematical singularities, the nature of dark matter/dark energy, and the hierarchy problem—by interpreting physical phenomena as entropy equilibration dynamics within a "4D Invariant Matrix Space."

### 1.2 Fundamental Axioms

1. **Invariance of Space (Invariant Matrix Space)**  
The true stage of the universe is a static, perfectly flat 4D Euclidean space (matrix space) that undergoes no expansion, contraction, curvature, or folding. The 3D space we perceive is merely the boundary surface of local energy wavefronts (ripples) propagating within this 4D space.
2. **Unification of Energy Density (Scalar Energy Field $u$)**  
All physical phenomena (matter, electric charge, gravity, light, time) reduce to inhomogeneities in the scalar energy density $u(\boldsymbol{x}, \tau)$ distributed across the matrix space and its wave propagation. Independent physical entities like "mass" or "force" do not exist; all are state changes in energy concentration.
3. **Entropy Equalization Principle**  
The matrix space possesses a natural restoring force that seeks to flatten local energy concentrations (high-density regions) and resolve inhomogeneities (maximizing entropy). This equilibration process propagates as "ripples (waves)."

## 2. Foundation Equations

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial \tau^2} + \gamma \frac{\partial u}{\partial \tau} = K(u) \nabla^2 u - \beta(\nabla u) \cdot u^2$$

* $u(\boldsymbol{x}, \tau)$: Scalar energy density in the 4D invariant matrix space.
* $\tau$: Absolute proper time in the matrix space (propagation parameter).
* $\gamma$: Base dissipation/attenuation coefficient of space (degree of wave damping).
* $K(u)$: Dynamic restoring coefficient (diffusion and restoring force of ripples).
* $\beta(\nabla u)$: Dynamic high-density self-interaction coupling constant (non-linear self-resistance / viscosity).
* $\nabla^2$: 4D Laplacian operator ($\frac{\partial^2}{\partial (x^1)^2} + \frac{\partial^2}{\partial (x^2)^2} + \frac{\partial^2}{\partial (x^3)^2} + \frac{\partial^2}{\partial (x^4)^2}$).

### 4.1 Inertia at Everyday Scales and Intuitive Examples

Inertia in this framework is not fictitious spacetime curvature, but **spatial resistance and response lag encountered when a moving body pushes through and excites energy ripples in the matrix space ahead.**

* **[Butterfly inside a moving train]**: The vast energy mountain composing the train carries the local matrix energy density $u$ along with it. The butterfly flies within this "locally adjusted energy field," exhibiting identical behavior to a stationary room, while from the outside, its motion is added to the wave motion of the train.
* **[The pushing feeling during car acceleration]**: When a car accelerates rapidly, the matrix energy density $u$ ahead of your body momentarily compresses, creating a steep gradient ($\nabla u$). The resistance from this "density wall ahead" combined with the counter-force from the "steep gradient" (gradient repulsive force) constitutes the true physical identity of the inertial force we feel daily.

### 4.2 Nature of Mass, Gravity, and Universal Gravitation
* **Mass**: Local concentration of energy density in the matrix space (solitons / standing waves).
* **Gravity**: Gentle gradient of energy density ($\nabla u$) radiated and diffused spherically by mass.
* **Derivation of Universal Gravitation**:  
Following spherical wave diffusion in 3D space, energy density at distance $r$ obeys $u(r) \propto \frac{1}{r}$. <br>
Multi-wave interference between two standing waves ($u_1, u_2$) generates an interactive force:
$$\boldsymbol{F}_{\text{gravity}} \propto - \nabla (u_1 \cdot u_2) \propto \frac{M_1 M_2}{r^2}$$

Newton's inverse-square law is thus naturally derived as a multi-wave interference effect of spherically diffusing ripples.
