## 2. The True Nature of Time: Why Was Time Treated Specially?

In modern physics, time is treated as something "special and entirely separate from space." However, this is merely an "illusion" and a "historical artifact" planted within us through human survival.

### 2.1 Four Reasons Why Time Has Been Treated Specially

1. **Survival Intuition (Space Allows Return, Time Does Not):** We can move freely back and forth or side to side, but time possesses an overwhelming physical sensation of moving in only one direction from past to future.


2. **Difference in Tools and Units:** It was convenient to create separate measuring tools—a ruler (meters / $m$) for space and a clock (seconds / $s$) for time—and to treat them in separate boxes for calculation purposes.


3. **Newtonian Absolute Space and Time:** In the 17th century, the approach of completely separating a "static giant stage (space)" and an "evenly ticking clock (time)" was overwhelmingly successful.


4. **Irreversibility:** Because time is unidirectional, it felt fundamentally distinct from space, where movement is free.

### 2.2 The Length of Space and the Length of Time Are Equivalent

This theory proposes that the length of space and the length of time are equivalent ($L = T$).

* **Time is not flowing:** Whether it is the hour between 11:00 and 12:00 or between 3:00 and 4:00, we are merely measuring the "interval (length) between point A and point B" along the time axis.


* **The True Nature of "Now":** The ripples generated at the moment of the universe's birth have expanded infinitely not only through space but also along the **time axis**. The leading edge of those ripples is the true nature of what we call "the present / now." Time is not flowing; rather, we mistake **"the spread of ripples propagating along the time axis"** for "the passage of time."
