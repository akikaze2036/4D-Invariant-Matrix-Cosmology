## Abstract

This paper presents a conceptual model that interprets physical phenomena as wave propagation of scalar energy density in a static 4D matrix space. Without relying on higher-dimensional strings or extra dimensions, this framework attempts to uniformly explain inertia, gravity, and particle formation using only an invariant matrix space and a dynamic field (energy density $u$). Inertial force is formally defined as the combined resistance of spatial "density walls" and "gradient repulsive forces" ($\nabla u$). This document is a concept model and prediction released for the purpose of open verification, theoretical refinement, and scholarly discourse.

---

## 1. Fundamental Postulates

1. **Invariance of Matrix Space:** Space itself does not expand, contract, or curve; it exists as a uniform, static, and flat 4D matrix.
2. **Energy Density Field:** All physical phenomena are described as inhomogeneities of scalar energy density $u(\mathbf{x}, t)$ within the matrix space and its equilibration dynamics (wave propagation).
3. **Nature of Elementary Particles:** Elementary particles are "wave vortices" (knots of localized standing waves) generated by steep gradients of energy density during early structural formation.

---

## 2. Foundation Equations

The propagation and equilibration of energy density in the matrix space are governed by the following **Extended Telegrapher's Equation** featuring dynamic parameters:

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} + \beta(u, \nabla u) \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = c^2 \nabla^2 u - K(u) u$$

Where:
* **$u(\mathbf{x}, t)$**: Scalar energy density at coordinate $\mathbf{x}$ and time $t$.
* **$K(u)$**: Dynamic restoring force function depending on the energy density level.
* **$\beta(u, \nabla u)$**: Dynamic self-resistance (viscosity) function depending on the steepness of the local energy gradient.

## 3. Interpretation of Physical Phenomena

### 3.1 Dual Structure of Inertial Force
Inertial force is defined as the sum of two dynamic spatial resistances:
1. **Density Wall Resistance:** Spatial resistance encountered when displacing the elevated energy density ahead of a moving object.
2. **Gradient Repulsive Force:** Counter-force derived from the steep energy density gradient ($\nabla u$) generated during acceleration.

$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{inertia}} = -\left( \alpha \cdot u_{\text{wall}} + \gamma \cdot \nabla u \right)$$

### 3.2 Nature of Gravity
Gravity is not the curvature of spacetime, but a phenomenon where objects (wave vortices) are pushed toward regions of lower energy density along the gradient ($\nabla u$) created in the surrounding matrix space by nearby matter.

### 3.3 Apparent Cosmic Expansion
Space itself is invariant. Cosmic expansion is an apparent phenomenon where energy ripples (matter and galaxies) expand and disperse within the static matrix space over absolute coordinate time.

## 4. Extreme States: Black Holes and Cosmological Predictions

Under extreme energy density $u$ and steep gradients $\nabla u$, the dynamic parameters $K(u)$ and $\beta(\nabla u)$ reach critical non-linear regimes:
* **Black Hole Interiors:** Extreme density leads to parameter saturation and potential spatial phase transitions (energy bounce), providing a mechanism for novel expansion seeds rather than physical singularities.
* **Wavefront Streamlining:** Theoretical potential exists for mitigating spatial resistance by controlling wave interference patterns, offering a conceptual pathway regarding energy barriers near light speed.
